## 4. Visual language

- **Headlines:** high-contrast serif, tight leading, sentence case. Body and UI: clean sans.
- **Canvas:** warm off-white. Section bands alternate subtle tints (pale blue-lavender, soft sand).
- **Cards:** white, 1px light border, generous padding, small radius. No shadows heavier than a soft lift.
- **Primary:** cobalt/teal blue — reserved for CTAs, eyebrows, badges, the hero caption bar.
- **Badges:** small pill outlines, uppercase, letterspaced.
- **Buttons:** solid cobalt for primary, thin outline for secondary. Small and precise, never full-width except the form CTA.
- **Stats:** oversized numerals as a visual anchor.
- **Photo:** real classroom imagery, documentary style — one per hero/CTA moment.

## 5. Non-negotiables

- No purple gradients, neon, or cold gray palettes.
- No generic SaaS cards floating on blobs.
- Serif display + warm neutral canvas is the signature — do not substitute a geometric sans for headings.
- Cobalt is an accent, not a flood. Keep the page predominantly warm off-white.
