Frequently Asked Questions & Transit Directions

Clear navigation instructions whether you are coming from Ikorodu Central, Benson, or commuting from outlying communities.

directions_bus

How do I get here from Ikorodu Garage / Benson?

From Ikorodu Garage, board a bus or tricycle heading toward Igbogbo / Igbe Road. Alight at Laara Bus Stop. From Laara Bus Stop, enter Banana Estate street opposite the Community Central Mosque; ADJ consultants is hosted within the Greater Heights complex 150 meters inwards.

explore Landmark: Central Mosque & Banana Gate
fact_check

What should students bring to the Diagnostic Session?

Please bring previous term results, mock reports, or prior JAMB/WAEC slips if available. Candidates should also bring writing materials for the brief 30-minute subject assessment. Calculators are provided inside our CBT Practice Lab.
