What is JUPEB?

The Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board (JUPEB) is an apex national academic examination board established by a consortium of leading Federal Universities and led by the University of Lagos.

The final certificate is formally approved by the Federal Ministry of Education and recognized by over 80 federal, state, and accredited private institutions across Nigeria.
