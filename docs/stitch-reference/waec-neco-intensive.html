warning Mathematics Penalties

Step-Marking Breakdown

WAEC awards M (Method), A (Accuracy), and B (Independent) marks. Omitting the standard algebraic sequence yields immediate 0 even if the final numerical answer is correct.

warning Physics Violations

Unit Omission Docks

Failure to append correct S.I. units (e.g., $N/m^2$, $ms^{-1}$) at the final step docks 1 full mark per question, resulting in cumulative grade degradation across Section B.

warning English Paper 1 & 2

Essay Structural Strictness

Rigid adherence to Content, Organization, Expression, and Mechanical Accuracy (COEM). Formal letters with punctuation flaws in recipient addresses are immediately penalized.

warning Biology Practical

Diagram Magnification

Diagrams must use clean un-feathered lines, clear label guidelines without arrowheads, explicit magnification titles ($\times 1.5$ to $\times 2.0$), and proportional accuracy.
