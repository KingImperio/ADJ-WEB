The 5 Hidden Pitfalls That Disqualify 270+ JAMB Candidates

Every admission cycle in Lagos State, thousands of ambitious scholars score well above 250 in the UTME yet end up without admission. The shortfall is rarely academic capacity—it is procedural and regulatory disqualification.

Pitfall 01 • Quota Ignorance pie_chart

Catchment & Federal Quota Misalignment

Federal universities distribute admissions via: 45% National Merit, 35% Catchment Area, and 20% Educationally Less Developed States (ELDS). Applying to institutions like UNILORIN or UNIBEN as an applicant from Lagos/Ogun without hitting pure national merit thresholds immediately surrenders your slot to local catchment candidates.

ADJ Strategic Safeguard: We cross-map candidate origin against institutional catchment criteria before JAMB change of institution locks down.
Pitfall 02 • Faculty Clash swap_horiz

Subject Combination Nuances

Faculty requirements differ subtly across institutions. For instance, UNILAG Accounting strictly rejects Commerce in place of Economics, while LASU permits particular commercial substitutions. A candidate scoring 290 with the wrong 4-subject combination faces immediate automated disqualification.

Safeguard: Line-by-line JAMB Interactive e-Brochure (IBASS) verification.
Pitfall 03 • System Timeouts hourglass_disabled

Ignored JAMB CAPS Marketplace

When candidates miss the departmental cutoff, universities often transfer them into allied courses via the CAPS marketplace. You have a strict 72 to 120-hour window to review and accept transfer proposals. Ignoring or misinterpreting this portal action results in permanent forfeiture.

Safeguard: Weekly profile audit and immediate SMS/WhatsApp prompt to parents upon CAPS status updates.
Pitfall 04 • Multi-Sitting Rules merge_type

O-Level Blending & Sitting Penalties

Blending WAEC and NECO is widely permitted, but premier faculties (e.g. UNILAG College of Medicine) strictly mandate ONE sitting with 5 distinctions. Combining sittings without assessing individual school weighting deduction equations leads to sudden rejection at the physical clearance phase.

Safeguard: Physical screening audit of statement of results prior to registration.
Pitfall 05 • Mathematical Oversight calculate

Underestimating Aggregate Formulas

Different institutions execute radically distinct composite weighting metrics: UNILAG uses 50% UTME + 30% Post-UTME CBT + 20% O-Level; LASU executes a pure 50:50 O-level point system alongside UTME score. Misunderstanding this causes candidates to falsely overestimate their competitive footing.

Safeguard: Exact composite simulation using current academic year university formulas.
