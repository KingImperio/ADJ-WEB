## Brand & Style

The visual narrative bridges collegiate institutional prestige with communal accessibility. Designed specifically for students, parents, and working professionals in Ikorodu, Lagos, the interface rejects cold, corporate distance in favor of an authoritative, structured, yet deeply encouraging environment. It communicates proven results, mentorship, and high-stakes examination mastery (JAMB UTME, WAEC/NECO, JUPEB, IELTS, SAT).

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Authoritative & Disciplined:** Evokes the credibility of premier academic halls through structured geometry, reliable alignments, and traditional typographic pairings.
- **Empowering & Optimistic:** Highlights student progress and milestones with luminous emerald accents and celebratory gold badges, reinforcing tangible academic breakthroughs.
- **Warm & Community-Rooted:** Welcoming language and accessible patterns ensure both digital-first secondary school leavers and aspirational parents navigating university admissions feel supported.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Modern Collegiate Editorial**: Crisp editorial type scales, structured informational borders, subtle surface lifts, and refined tabular metrics. The aesthetic eliminates frivolous neon gradients and hyper-glass effects in favor of crisp legibility, distinct informational hierarchy, high-contrast badges, and tactile credibility.

## Colors

The palette balances the heritage of traditional educational institutions with vigorous accents denoting growth and distinction.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#1A365D`):** Anchors major navigation bars, primary calls-to-action, section headers, and core brand structural markers. Represents institutional integrity and disciplined study.
- **Secondary Forest Emerald (`#0D7A53`):** Conveys growth, verified admission statuses, positive score outcomes, and success metrics. Applied across conversion triggers, progress indicators, and active course tracks.
- **Tertiary Amber Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved for honor ribbons, high-score distinctions, top percentile indicators, critical notices, and scholarship badges.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The foundational color for long-form body text, instructional materials, and headings, ensuring maximum contrast and legibility under direct sunlight on mobile screens.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** Off-white/slate tint (`#F8FAFC`) to minimize glare while remaining crisp and clean.
- **Card/Surface Fill:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for distinct content demarcation.
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** Muted Navy/Slate (`#F1F5F9`) for secondary card containers, table headers, and mock-exam review modules.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle slate-slate borders (`#E2E8F0`) providing clear structural bounds without visual weight.

## Typography

The type system blends the institutional dignity of classical scholarly journals with high-legibility contemporary digital layouts.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Source Serif 4`):** Delivers authority, distinction, and classic academic rigor. Reserved for main titles, testimonial pull quotes, and major milestones.
- **Interface, Metrics & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine legibility across mobile devices, schedule tables, examination forms, and interactive syllabus trees.
- **Numbers & Statistical Indicators:** Stat numbers inherit weights of `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular figures enabled (`tnum`) to maintain exact visual alignment in registration pricing, score leaderboards, and percentage pass rates.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 8pt baseline rhythm governs spacing to maintain an organized, classroom-like order across all screen sizes.

### Breakpoints & Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Single-column stack for test packages, registration wizards, and contact details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables 2-column comparative matrices for syllabus tracks and score trends.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1240px` with `margin-desktop: 4rem` and `gutter-desktop: 2rem`. Allows side-by-side informational panels, consultation scheduling, and comprehensive exam badge filters.

### Component Spacing Rules
- Form field stacks and list items use `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px) gaps.
- Structural card internal padding uses `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` (24px) on desktop.
- Academic module sections maintain `space-xxl` (64px) separators to prevent cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids decorative, floaty drop shadows in favor of grounded, functional depth that feels structured and concrete.

### Tiers & Elevation Style
- **Flat Ground (Surface Tier 0):** Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with no shadow.
- **Structured Card (Surface Tier 1):** Pure white background bounded by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` with a subtle ground shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Card Hover & Floating Element (Surface Tier 2):** Subtle lift using a directed shadow with slate-tinted ambient spread: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(26, 54, 93, 0.03)`, accompanied by a primary border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Modals, Overlays & Sticky Navbars (Surface Tier 3):** Crisp elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` with a solid background and hairline bottom border.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a disciplined geometric posture (`roundedness: 1` — Soft / 4px base radius). 

### Form Factor Application
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Select Boxes:** Soft 4px (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`) corners conveying stability and academic precision.
- **Cards, Modals, and Dashboard Containers:** 8px (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`) corners ensuring content blocks feel contained without rounded softness that undermines formality.
- **Badges, Tags, and Pill Counters:** Strict 4px rounded rectangles (`rounded-sm`) rather than continuous capsules or full pills, matching the utilitarian stamp of official exam certificates and admission slips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A365D`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px border radius. Hover state transitions to `#132946` with Tier 1 elevation. Height: 44px (touch-friendly on mobile).
- **Secondary (Achievement/Registration):** Background `#0D7A53`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for high-intent actions like "Enroll for JAMB Clinic" or "Book Consultation". Hover state transitions to `#0A5C3E`.
- **Outline/Tertiary:** 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text `#1A365D`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active and focus states highlight with `#1A365D` outline.

### Exam Badges
Compact, bold identifiers displaying test categories (JAMB, WAEC, NECO, JUPEB, IELTS, SAT):
- **JAMB / UTME:** Emerald base — `#ECFDF5` background, `#0D7A53` border and text.
- **WAEC / NECO:** Navy base — `#EFF6FF` background, `#1A365D` border and text.
- **JUPEB / Direct Entry:** Indigo base — `#EEF2FF` background, `#3730A3` border and text.
- **IELTS / SAT:** Amber base — `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` border and text.
- Badges feature all-caps, tracking `0.04em`, 11px font size, and 2px horizontal padding with 4px corner radii.

### Cards
- **Program & Exam Cards:** White background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, padding 24px. Features the exam badge at top-left, course title in `Source Serif 4`, bulleted curriculum breakdown, schedule indicator, and bottom CTA button.
- **Community Proof & Testimonial Cards:** Features student name, local secondary school or Ikorodu neighborhood cue (e.g., "Ikorodu Town Hall Center", "Agric-Ikorodu Alum"), verified score badge (e.g., "318 in UTME"), and portrait photo with a 1px border.

### Stats Indicators
- High-contrast stat blocks with large numerical values in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold (36px), paired with an uppercase label in `label-sm` (`#64748B`). Often framed with a vertical left accent border (3px solid `#0D7A53` or `#D97706`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Standard text fields have an off-white fill (`#F8FAFC`), 1px neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), 4px corner radius, and 44px minimum touch height.
- Focus state switches to pure white background with a 2px stroke in `#1A365D` and zero ambient glow. Error states utilize `#DC2626` with explicit micro-copy underneath.

### Local Context Indicators
- Geolocation and landmark chips (e.g., "Main Center: Beside General Hospital Road, Ikorodu", "Weekend Mock Center: Ebute-Ikorodu") rendered with small location icon indicators in `#0D7A53` to ground the consultancy in its physical community.
